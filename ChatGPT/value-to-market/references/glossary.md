# Canonical Glossary

Use these terms exactly. Precision here is what separates the method from generic value-prop advice.

## Promise anatomy
- **Trigger Clause** - the IF condition that activates the promise.
- **Promise Object** - what is actually promised.
- **Qualified Recipient** - the chosen target inside the promise sentence (not the whole market).
- **Enforcer** - the self-reflected distinctive adjective that makes the promise unmistakably yours.
- **Promise Conditions** - the perimeter of fulfilment; may include an optional rolling pool.
- **Empty Promise** - a promise resting on no real value; corrosive, it nullifies the whole bracket.
- **Air-Inflated Promise** - a high Ladder rung declared without the rungs beneath it.
- **Expectations Gap** - Promise minus Proof; the gap proof exists to hold at zero.
- **Whiplash** - the wound from a refuted proof; trust takes the minimum, not the average.

## Values
- **Antithesis** - a value you deliberately reject; premise in its purest form, a filter at the door.
- **Tangibilization** - giving an abstract benefit a perceivable body; stabilizes a value and consumes it in the same act.

## Audience and channels
- **Toxic Customer** - a customer whose relationship destroys value; defined by who they are, their behaviors, and why they cost more than they yield.
- **Spillover** - value or incoherence leaking across channels/touchpoints; multichannel without spillover is the goal.

## Markets
- **Category King** - the owner of a category; captures ~76% of the category's value.
- **Zero-Billion Market** - a category that does not yet exist: no king, no rules, no reference prices; the best kingdom.

## Defense
- **Court** - the density of services and relationships around an imitable asset; either too numerous to replace as a block, or themselves distinctive.

## Measurement
- **Perceived Value** - value the customer actually registers; the only value that counts strategically.
- **Willingness to Pay and to Sell** - the top notch (WTP) and bottom notch (WTS) of the value stick.
- **Customer Delight** - the WTP-minus-price slice; a reserve to spend deliberately.
- **Value Inflection Points** - where value shifts the buyer's reference point.
- **Metric Decay Paradox** - metrics proliferate, decorrelate from outcomes, and decay (Meyer & Gupta); dashboards rot.

## Power
- **The Five Power Approaches** - the strategic approaches the ten figures express behaviorally.

## Going to market
- **Seven Wastes of Value** - undeclared, unproven, delivered-to-those-who-don't-appreciate, late, diluted-in-handover, contradicted, uncollected.
- **Value Attribution / Value Bandwagoning** - the two maneuvers of value evolution.

## Portfolio over time (Rhyming)
- **Singleton** - the unit of the portfolio: one champion offering plus the bounded ecosystem of supporting products, services and experiences around it. Fractal (each dot is also a full score), bounded but not sealed (Singletons overlap without dissolving). Note: collides with the software design pattern of the same name.
- **String** - a connected sequence of dots, one line of development.
- **Movement** - a phrase of one to four notes that hangs together as a recognizable unit.
- **Beat** - the separation marking where a movement repeats.
- **Tempo** - the speed of the organization: how fast the beats come. Internal (own cadence) versus market tempo.
- **Internal rhyme / external rhyme** - does the move extend our own architecture, and does it land in time with the market. Both are required.
- **Sustain / chord / rest / platform mark** - a repeated line, notes launched together, a beat with no launch, a Service or Commodity opened for others (`*`).

## Systems
- **Shi and Non-Additivity** - 1+1 does not equal 2; shi (势, propensity) vs li (力, force); conflict makes 1+1=0, synergy makes 1+1=10.

## Craft
- **Performance Zone and Learning Zone** (Briceño) - stay a white belt; best practices go stale.

# Power Shifting

Power organizes reality; in branding it looks like normality. The danger is not weak power, it is confused power.

## On invocation
Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/output-protocol.md`, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/frameworks.md` (Power Shifting, Power Timeline, Asymmetry of Power), `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scorecards.md`. Ask the brand, its market condition and maturity, and rivals to contrast.

## Procedure
1. Read the market condition, then the figure. The ten by phase: Emblem, Illustrator, Affect display; Regulator, Adaptor; Stance, Steeple, Proxemics; Orientation, Mirroring.
2. Executed figure vs intended figure; would customers name the same one?
3. Confused-power scan across touchpoints.
4. Temporal drift: inspiring in year one, manipulative in year five.
5. **Suggest the best posture combinations for this case.** Figures are rarely played alone; propose the two or three that reinforce each other for this brand and condition (for example Stance plus Steeple to hold conviction and authority, or Orientation plus Mirroring to lead by identity), name the dominant and the supporting, and flag combinations that would read as confused power together.
6. **Build a posture scenario map.** Lay out how and when to shift posture as the market moves: for each plausible market event, the **signal** that it is happening, the **trigger** threshold that calls for a move, the posture combination to adopt, and the one first gesture. Cover the obvious shifts (a rival attacks, the category matures, a crisis hits, scale changes the power). These signals and triggers are written to be **reused in the validation plan** (`v2m-set-6-engineer`) as watched conditions, so the posture move fires on evidence, not mood.

## Recommendation
The gesture to commit to now, the best posture combination for the case, the confused-power signals to fix, the posture scenario map (signal, trigger, posture, first gesture), and for a rival the outframe. Score against the Power scorecard.

Follow `output-protocol.md` for the reasoning file, self-score, and next step. Always produce the branded Word report, and since this tool is list-heavy always generate a branded Excel: a figure-read tab (market condition, executed vs intended figure, confused-power findings, drift), a combinations tab (dominant plus supporting, why it coheres), and a scenario-map tab (market event, signal, trigger threshold, posture combination, first gesture, carried-to-validation yes/no).

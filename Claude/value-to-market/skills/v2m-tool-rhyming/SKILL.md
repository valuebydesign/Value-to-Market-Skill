# Rhyming

See growth as music: whether the moves compound or merely accumulate. Winners extend, losers orphan. Rhyme, do not copy.

## On invocation
Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/output-protocol.md`, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/rhyming.md` in full, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scorecards.md`. Ask for the launch history and the candidate next moves; mark 2025-2026 moves as photographs to refresh.

## Procedure
1. Draw the staff; plot each launch as a Singleton with the classification rubric.
2. Notation string and ASCII staff with outcome glyphs.
3. Name the movement, mark beats, measure tempo.
4. Match to the six patterns or six antipatterns.
5. Rhyme-Check Scorecard and Next-Note Selector on each candidate; reject any zero on internal or external rhyme.
6. Antipattern pre-mortem and portfolio-balance check.
7. **Draw the Singleton map for the moment and its evolution across the horizons.** First the map as it stands today. Then, per horizon (H1/H2/H3), decide the move on the staff: **add a Singleton** (a new note that rhymes), **substitute one** (retire a note and replace it with a rhyming successor), or **remove one** (drop an orphan that no longer compounds). Show the map evolving horizon by horizon, not as one static snapshot.
8. **Compose the rhyming propositions, each anchored to a famous historical case.** Offer several distinct ways the next move could rhyme, and ground each in a specific, well-known business precedent whose pattern was later copied by other brands (for example the razor-and-blades tie, the halo-product-then-mass-market descent, the platform-then-first-party move, the ingredient-brand alliance). Name the case, the pattern it established, and who copied it, so the proposition stands on a proven pattern rather than a hunch.

## Recommendation
A **recommended next move** plus two or three **secondaries**, so that if the user expresses no preference we proceed with the recommended one. For each: the Singleton, its note and beat, the pattern it extends, the horizon map move (add / substitute / remove), the historical case it rhymes with, and the rationale. Be honest: strong diagnosis, weak predictor; attach a confidence and the counter-case.

Follow `output-protocol.md` for the reasoning file, self-score, and next step. Always produce the branded Word report, and since this tool is list-heavy always generate a branded Excel: a Singleton-map tab (moment plus one column per horizon showing the add/substitute/remove move), and a propositions tab (proposition, recommended or secondary, pattern, historical case, who copied it, confidence, rationale).

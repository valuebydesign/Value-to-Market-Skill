# Value to Market Tactics

The market is wilderness, not an arena. Win without fighting: a fight is the most expensive way to gain ground.

## On invocation
Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/output-protocol.md`, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/method-core.md`, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/frameworks.md`, `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scorecards.md`. Ask position (challenger or incumbent), pursuers, assets in hand, and the objective.

## Procedure
1. Name the priority: get to value, shake pursuers, gain advantage.
2. **Condition the category first.** Pull the category (Frame, Name, Claim) from `v2m-tool-category-design`; the launch enters a frame, not a void. Decide what the market must already believe on the day of the big event, and which claim the launch makes the market's default.
3. **Define the beachhead with the user.** The smallest, winnable segment where the value dominates and word travels: who exactly, why them first, what "won" looks like, and the crossing signal that says the beachhead is held and expansion can begin. Do not spread the launch across the whole market; take the beachhead, then move.
4. Choose the field-manual moves: Win Without Fighting, Running Toward Value, Taking Off, Divergence, Evacuation, Line Breaking, Stealth, Weaponizing, Area Exploitation, Fighting When You Must, Protecting Others, Synergy.
5. **Plan the launch sequence: three strikes before the big event.** The big event (the reveal, the on-sale, the keynote) is a lightning strike, not a drip; it lands only if the ground is prepared. Sequence three preparatory strikes leading into it, each with its single best action to implement:
   - **Strike 1 - condition** (frame and audience): make the market believe the category thesis before the product exists. Best action: the one move that seeds the frame (a manifesto, a data point, a partner's endorsement).
   - **Strike 2 - prove** (evidence and desire): show the promise is real and stoke wanting. Best action: the one costly proof or demonstration that converts skeptics.
   - **Strike 3 - mobilize** (allies and pre-commitment): line up the ecosystem, the waitlist, the pre-orders, the launch-day amplifiers so the big event opens with momentum, not silence. Best action: the one pre-commitment that guarantees a full room on day zero.
   - **The big event**: the concentrated reveal. Name the date, the single message, and the one metric that says it landed.
   For each strike: the timing relative to the big event, the owner, the one best action, and the signal that it worked before advancing to the next.
6. **Kickstart the launch copy and content.** Draft the launch messaging and the per-strike, per-channel content that fires the sequence: the manifesto/frame piece, the proof asset, the mobilize call. Hand this to `v2m-set-6-engineer` to stand up as an automated content engine wired to the launch calendar.
7. **Plan market expansion on three horizons, with triggers.** Beyond the beachhead, go deep on the logical three-horizon path and make each move contingent, not calendar-bound:
   - **H1 - hold the beachhead** (win and defend the first segment), **H2 - adjacent expansion** (the next segments or geographies the beachhead unlocks), **H3 - the new curve** (the offering the current one makes possible).
   - **Triggers and offering evolution.** For each horizon, name the trigger condition (a metric threshold, a rival move, a regulatory or technology shift) and the scenario it activates. A trigger can fire **a change of core values**, **an evolution of an asset**, or **the launch of a competitor line-up** (a fighter or flanking offer). Build the scenario practically: if trigger X fires, then this move, this owner, this cost, this signal it worked.
   - Give a recommendation per horizon: the most likely scenario and the pre-decided response, so the offering evolves on evidence rather than by guess. The trigger conditions hand off to `v2m-set-6-engineer` to be instrumented and watched automatically.
8. Audit the seven wastes of value before spending.
9. Viability read: cost, exposure, reversibility.

## Recommendation
The recommended tactic(s) with the real-company precedent, the move stated concretely (steps, danger radius or minimum distance, the signal to watch), the conditioned category the launch enters, the beachhead, the three-strike launch sequence into the big event with its kickstart content, and the three-horizon expansion with its triggers and scenario responses. Route to `v2m-set-6-engineer`.

Follow `output-protocol.md` for the reasoning file, self-score, next step, and the branded deliverables (Word by default, Excel where list-heavy).

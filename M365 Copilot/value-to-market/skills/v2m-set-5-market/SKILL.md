# v2m-set-5-market - Value to Market

Carry the designed value into the market without dispersing it. The market is wilderness; win without fighting.

## On invocation
Read `../../references/output-protocol.md`, `../../references/method-core.md`, `../../references/frameworks.md`. Pull the design and strategy, and the category (Frame, Name, Claim) the launch will enter.

## Chain
1. `v2m-tool-value-to-market-tactics` - condition the category the launch enters, define the beachhead with the user, name the priority (get to value / shake pursuers / gain advantage), choose the moves, plan the launch sequence (three strikes before the big event, each with its single best action, into the concentrated reveal), kickstart the launch copy and content, and plan market expansion on three horizons with triggers and scenario responses.
2. Audit the seven wastes of value before spending.

## Gate
Score the plan for viability (cost, exposure, reversibility). The launch sequence passes only if each of the three strikes has an owner, a best action, and a signal to confirm before the next, and the big event has a date, a single message, and a landing metric. The beachhead must be named with its crossing signal, and each expansion horizon must have a trigger and a pre-decided response. Hand the kickstart content and the horizon triggers to `v2m-set-6-engineer` to automate and instrument. Recommend `v2m-set-6-engineer`.

## Output
Per `output-protocol.md`: `v2m-set-5-market-<subject>-<date>.md` with the conditioned category, the beachhead, the tactics, the three-strike launch sequence into the big event, the kickstart launch content, the three-horizon expansion with triggers and scenario responses, the wastes audit, and the next step. Produce the branded phase deliverables per report-format.md: a Word report, a PowerPoint deck and a PDF of the phase, and an Excel where the phase is list-heavy (a strike-by-strike launch timeline: strike, timing before the big event, best action, owner, confirming signal; and a horizon-and-trigger table: horizon, trigger condition, scenario activated, response move, owner, signal).

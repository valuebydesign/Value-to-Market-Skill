# Category Assessment

Before you design a category you must read the ones that already shape the customer's expectations. Every category carries a set of expected values: meet the hygiene ones or you are not considered, and the differentiating ones are where the game is actually played. This is diagnostic (read the expectations); `v2m-tool-category-design` is generative (build the frame).

## On invocation
Read `../../references/output-protocol.md`, `../../references/frameworks.md` (Category Design, Three Oceans, Category Value Map), `../../references/scorecards.md`. Load `.value-to-market/context.md`. Confirm the line of business and the category the customer files you under; search the web if enabled.

## Procedure

1. **Map the category rings** (nearest to farthest):
   - **Specific** - the category or industry you are in.
   - **Adjacent** - neighbours sharing customers, jobs or occasions.
   - **Similar** - a different market with the same structure (analogous), a source of moves not yet imported.
   - **Overlapping** - categories that share some attributes, customers or moments with yours.
   - **Loosely related** - far from your category, few things in common, mined for transferable ideas.
2. **Read the expected values** per ring: what customers take for granted (hygiene / table stakes), what differentiates today, and what is emerging. Place them on the five value lenses (functional, emotional, social, ethical, transformational).
3. **Classify each value**: hygiene (must meet), differentiator (where to win), or fading (tangibilized into a standard, per Transient Stability).
4. **Spot the transfers**: values or moves an adjacent, similar, overlapping or loosely related category has that yours has not yet imported.

## Recommendation
The hygiene values you must meet, the differentiators worth contesting, the fading ones to stop over-investing in, and the two or three transferable moves to import from other rings. Feeds `v2m-tool-category-design` and `v2m-tool-core-values`.

## Output
Follow `output-protocol.md`. List-heavy, so also build a comprehensive branded Excel divided into tabs: one tab per ring (Specific, Adjacent, Similar, Overlapping, Loosely related), each row a value with columns for lens, hygiene/differentiator/fading, evidence, and transferable-to-us; plus a summary tab of hygiene-to-meet, differentiators-to-contest, and moves-to-import. Filters on.

# Jobs To Be Done

People do not buy products, they hire them to make progress. A job is progress in a circumstance, not a demographic and not a feature.

## On invocation
Read `../../references/output-protocol.md`, `../../references/customer-fit.md`, `../../references/frameworks.md`, `../../references/scorecards.md`. Ask the line of business, the reference subjects and audiences, and the circumstances that matter (and in B2B the decision-maker vs the user).

## Procedure
1. **Three levels** - for each job write the functional (task), social (how they want to be seen), and emotional (how they want to feel) reading.
2. **Audiences and cases** - run the set per reference subject and per concrete case, since the same product is hired for different jobs by different people.
3. **Modifiers** - stress-test each job under a constraint that changes what good means: under stress, tight time, limited resources, limited knowledge or visibility. A job under stress is often a different job.
4. **Objectives** - tag each job: personal gain, task, business or company advantage, or greater call.
5. Call out the high-leverage jobs: important and underserved.
6. **Cluster the jobs into groups (job maps) and rank each group against the strategy.** Group related jobs into a handful of named clusters (by outcome, occasion, or objective) so the set reads as a map, not a list. Present the groups to the user and ask each to be marked **Core** (central to the business strategy, design around it), **Important** (serve it well, not the priority), or **Irrelevant** (out of scope, consciously excluded). Carry the rating forward so the archetypes, canvas and features weight the Core groups first.

## Recommendation
The recommended job set: three-level jobs multiplied by audience/case and modifier, tagged by objective, clustered into groups each rated Core / Important / Irrelevant with the user, and the two or three highest-leverage jobs named. Feeds `v2m-tool-archetype-map` and `v2m-tool-value-proposition-canvas` (which weight the Core groups first), and is reused downstream to build the enhanced customer journey and to test the prototype. Score against the Jobs To Be Done scorecard.

Follow `output-protocol.md` for the reasoning file, self-score, next step, and the branded deliverables (Word by default, Excel where list-heavy).

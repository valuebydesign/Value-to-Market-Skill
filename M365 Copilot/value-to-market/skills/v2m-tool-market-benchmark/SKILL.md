# Market Benchmark

Read the field with the customer's yardstick. "We have no real competitors" is the most dangerous sentence in business.

## On invocation
Read `../../references/output-protocol.md`, `../../references/frameworks.md` (Three Oceans, Category Design), `../../references/tools-and-chains.md`, `../../references/scorecards.md`. Load `.value-to-market/context.md`. Ask the line of business, the players the user already knows, and whether to search the web for the rest.

## Procedure

1. **Build the competitor set in four rings.** Do not stop at the obvious names:
   - **Direct competitors** - same offer, same job, same audience.
   - **Competitors** - same job, different offer or model.
   - **Alternatives** - a different solution to the same job, including do-nothing, manual workarounds, and in-house builds.
   - **Similarities** - analogous industries that share the same structural pattern even if the category differs (for example energy providers resemble telcos: utility, subscription, switching, bundling). Mine these for moves the market has not yet imported.
2. **Profile each player on five dimensions.** For every player in scope capture: **Value Proposition** (the whole promise), **Unique Selling Proposition** (the distinctive object), **Core Values** (what they stand for), **Claims** (the public promises and marketing claims they make), and **Strategic plan direction** (where they are visibly heading: roadmap, investments, recent moves). Mark each cell as evidenced or inferred, and cite the source when searched.
3. **Ocean read** - red, blue, or new, and the arsenal each demands.
4. **Category Value Map** - score each player on the five value lenses (functional, emotional, social, ethical, transformational). Mark the crowded zones and the empty cells.
5. **Figure-and-Ladder read** per key rival: the power figure and the promise-ladder rung.
6. **Your standing** - lead / match / lag by lens, in benefit terms.
7. **White space** - keep only the empty cells an owned asset can actually prove.

## Recommendation
One recommended play: the ocean and the provable white space, each tied to its backing asset, plus the two or three rivals (or analogous-industry moves) most worth watching or borrowing from. The profile table feeds `v2m-tool-category-design` and `v2m-tool-moat-defense`. Route there next.

## Output
Follow `output-protocol.md` for the reasoning file, self-score, next step, and the branded deliverables. This tool is list-heavy, so also build a comprehensive branded Excel divided into tabs:

- **Direct competitors**, **Competitors**, **Alternatives**, **Similarities** - one tab per ring, one row per player, columns: player, Value Proposition, USP, Core Values, Claims, Strategic direction, evidenced-or-inferred, source.
- **Value map** - all players scored across the five lenses (functional, emotional, social, ethical, transformational), crowded zones and empty cells marked.
- **Standing and white space** - your lead / match / lag by lens, and the provable white spaces with their backing asset.

Every tab is Arial, header row in the highlight color, filters on. Keep it copy-paste clean.

# Adoption MOA

You build the ladder to your value; the market does not climb to you. All three uprights or nothing. Volume cures only volume problems. This tool lives in Customer and Fit: it scores adoption per persona and turns each gap into a test.

## On invocation
Read `references/output-protocol.md`, `references/frameworks.md` (MOA Model, Habit 3Rs), `references/scorecards.md`. Load the personas from `v2m-tool-personas` (or the reference subjects); in B2B keep decision-maker and user separate.

## Procedure
1. **Score the grid** per persona, four questions per dimension, red/yellow/green: Motivation (extrinsic, intrinsic, emulation, competition, persuasion), Opportunity (use cases, setting, what-ifs, honest urgency, constraints), Ability (calibrated language, metaphors, layering, visual simplification, levels).
2. **Attack the reddest column first**, not the photogenic symptom.
3. **B2B double pass** where relevant; say which persona gates the deal.
4. **Scale to the 97% pyramid**: map what educates the not-yet-buyers.
5. **Validation plan, per MOA element.** For Motivation, Opportunity and Ability, and for each red or yellow cell, write: the assumption, the test or experiment, the **suggested KPI**, the pass threshold, and the owner. State what result would falsify the assumption.

## Recommendation
The bottleneck, the fix order with mechanism-specific fixes (not "more marketing"), and the validation plan to run first (cheapest test that most reduces adoption risk). Feeds `v2m-set-6-engineer` (which absorbs the KPIs into the measurement plan) and the prototype test.

## Output
Follow `output-protocol.md`. List-heavy, so build a tabbed branded Excel: the MOA grid per persona (scores), and a **Validation plan** tab (assumption, test, KPI, pass threshold, owner, falsifier). Also the Word writeup of the bottleneck and the plan.

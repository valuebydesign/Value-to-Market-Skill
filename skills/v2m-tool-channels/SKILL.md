# Channels

Every pairing of proposition and value is aimed at a specific group in a specific channel. One-channel-fits-all must be a choice, never a default.

## On invocation
Read `references/output-protocol.md`, `references/frameworks.md` (Value Proposition Formula, Value Sequence and Value Network), `references/tools-and-chains.md`, `references/scorecards.md`. Pull the promise, recipient and values from the design tools. Ask the current channels and touchpoints.

## Procedure
1. Census: every channel and touchpoint.
2. Triplet map: bind each promise as value x recipient x channel.
3. Spillover check: where value or incoherence leaks; where a channel contradicts the promise.
4. **Unconventional channels.** Beyond the obvious channels, propose non-conventional ones that outcompete rivals who all crowd the same few: channels competitors ignore, borrowed from analogous industries (see the market benchmark's similarities ring), partner and ecosystem channels, community and creator routes, product-as-channel and embedded distribution. For each, name the audience it reaches (existing or new), why it is hard for a rival to follow, and the value x recipient x channel triplet it would carry.
5. Repair list, ordered by damage.
6. Channel briefs: one per channel (conventional and unconventional) for engineering.
7. **Update the business model canvas.** Feed the chosen channels into the BMC channels and customer-relationship blocks, and flag any new key partnership an unconventional channel requires; hand this to `v2m-tool-business-model-canvas` so the compiled model reflects the channel plan.

## Recommendation
The recommended triplet map, the unconventional channels to test with their target or new audiences, the top repairs, the channel briefs, and the BMC updates. Feeds `v2m-set-6-engineer` and `v2m-tool-business-model-canvas`. Score against the Channels scorecard.

Follow `output-protocol.md` for the reasoning file, self-score, and next step. Always produce the branded Word report, and since this tool is list-heavy always generate a branded Excel: a triplet-map tab (value, recipient, channel, coherence), an unconventional-channels tab (channel, audience reached, why hard to follow, triplet), a repair tab ordered by damage, and a BMC-updates tab (block, entry, new partnership needed).

# Guidelines

The rules that keep the strategy consistent across the people who execute it. A value proposition fails late, when scale moves the power while the language stays the same.

## On invocation
Read `references/output-protocol.md`, `references/frameworks.md`, `references/customer-fit.md`, `references/report-format.md`. Read the reasoning files, especially the verbalization (the brand/comms seeds). Ask who the guidelines are for and how strict.

## Structure
The values (with the antithesis: what we deliberately are not); the premise (who we serve, who we turn away, the courteous farewell); the promise wording and rung, with the enforcer; the proof the team must always show; the power figure to hold and the confused-power signals to avoid; tone of voice (do and do-not pairs); brand personality; mission, vision and purpose; do and do-not phrasing examples; the triggers that would change a value.

## Output
A guidelines document, always produced as a branded Word (via the docx skill) per `report-format.md`, saved into the project folder (never the vault). Rules with examples, not theory. Follow `output-protocol.md`.

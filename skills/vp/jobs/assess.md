# Job: Assess a value proposition

Tell the user how good a value proposition is, for whom, against whom, and what its weaknesses cost. The subject can be the user's own proposition, a rival's, a set of players side by side, or a whole category.

## Variants

| Variant | Typical input | Subject of the diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Ours | our website, our folder, a pasted pitch | the user's value proposition |
| A2 One rival | a competitor's website or name | that competitor's value proposition |
| A3 The field | a list of websites, "my 5 competitors", ours plus rivals | every player, side by side, the user's included if given |
| A4 The category | an industry or category name | the category itself: its players, expectations and open water |

## Intake

Read every source first (`engine/intake.md`). Then ask only what is open from the Assess questions: subject, whose yardstick, comparison set, purpose. For A3 and A4, propose the player set and let the user edit it before reading the sites.

## Modules

| Depth | A1 Ours | A2 One rival | A3 The field | A4 The category |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quick | source-read, vp-audit | source-read, vp-audit | source-read per player, benchmark (lite: profiles and value map) | benchmark (lite), category-scan |
| Standard | + benchmark, category-scan | + benchmark (rival's view) | + vp-audit per player, category-scan | + source-read of the leaders, vp-audit of the leader |
| Full | + assets (visible and declared), power | + power, defense (rival's moat) | + power per key rival | + power and ladder read per key player |

Notes:
- In A1, the assets module runs only on what the sources show plus what the user states; mark the rest as unknown. It is a first read, owned later by Design.
- In A3, run `vp-audit` with the same yardstick for every player so the scores compare. The diagnosis report then holds one section per player and a comparison section.
- In A2, write the diagnosis as the user's competitive read: where the rival is strong, where it is exposed, and what that opens for the user.

## Checkpoints

1. After reading the sources: confirm the player set and the yardstick, and share the off-the-cuff verbalization of the subject's proposition ("This is what your site says you are. Is that what you mean?").
2. Before writing: share the 3P headline and the reddest structural challenge, and ask whether anything the sources cannot show would change it.

## Reports

| Report | A1 | A2 | A3 | A4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value Statement (as-is) | yes | yes (the rival's) | Standard and Full (one per player) | no |
| 0 Executive Summary | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 Market and Category Landscape | Standard and Full | Standard and Full | yes | yes |
| 2 Value Proposition Diagnosis | yes | yes | yes (comparative) | Standard and Full (the leader) |
| 3 Foundations | Full (first read of assets) | no | no | no |

The Decisions and Agenda table in the Executive Summary holds the fixes ranked by cost of the gap, and the quick wins.

## Gate

The job is done when the 3P headline, the reddest structural challenge with its cost, and the fastest quick win are stated and independently checked. Report a low score as the result; the job is still complete.

## Hand-off

Recommend the next job following `engine/commands.md`, section Next step: `/vp design` when the diagnosis says to rebuild, `/vp evolve` when the proposition is sound but drifting, `/vp strategy` (the Value Strategy job) when the landscape shows the category itself is the problem.

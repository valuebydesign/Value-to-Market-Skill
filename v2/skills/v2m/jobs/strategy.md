# Job: Build a value strategy

The company-level job that decides what the company stands for and where it goes. It reads the field, settles what the company owns and stands for, designs the category and the defense, builds the value proposition of each line of business, and sets the scenarios the strategy must survive. It can run with a single analyst (you) or in agentic mode with parallel agents (`engine/agentic.md`).

## Scope

This job takes the strategic decisions: category, values, premise, promise and proof, defense, posture, the next moves on the staff, and the futures to prepare for. It names the beachhead and the horizons as choices and leaves their execution to Value to Market, under the boundary rule in `SKILL.md`. When the user asks for the whole journey, run this job, then hand over to Value to Market in the same session.

## Intake

Read the sources, then ask what is open from the Strategy questions in `engine/intake.md`: scope and lines of business, horizon and ambition, existing work, decision makers and dates, and agentic mode on or off with its budget. Reuse every earlier job found in `Knowledge/`.

## The plan

Start with the Stage Assessment (`method/frameworks.md`, Instruments) so the plan begins where the project really is, and skip what `Knowledge/` shows as settled. Write `plan.md` in the run folder: the modules in order, grouped by report, with the checkpoint after each group. Size it with the 6% rule from `method/method-core.md`: the strategy and its engineering should take about 6% of the total project budget; state the delivery horizon this implies. Cadence: 3 days to align, 12 weeks to design and equip, 6 months to show effects.

## Modules

| Group | Modules | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Field | source-read (rivals), benchmark, category-scan, change-scan | in agentic mode: the scouts |
| Foundations | business-sketch, assets, core-values | company level, once |
| Company strategy | category-design, value-matrix, defense, power, rhyming | in agentic mode: the architects draft rival strategies here and through Design |
| Per line of business | jobs, archetypes (Full), personas, canvas, adoption, 3p, ladder, pricing, channels, formula, verbalization | once per line in scope |
| Evolution | scenarios | in agentic mode: the scenario builder and the wind tunnel |
| Model | bmc | per audience and per horizon |
| Packaging | guidelines and deck (Full) | |

Depth: Quick runs Field, Foundations, Company strategy and a single-line Design without customer modules. Standard runs everything except archetypes, guidelines and deck. Full runs everything.

## Gates

- Do not design any line of business while core values or the category are below pass.
- Do not hand over to Value to Market while a line's Formula has a factor near zero, or while the scenarios lack signposts and pre-decided responses.

## Checkpoints

Single-analyst mode: after Field (the rivals and the ocean), after Foundations (sign the values), after Company strategy (the category and the defense), after each line's 3P, and before writing. Agentic mode uses the three checkpoints in `engine/agentic.md` and adds the values sign-off after the inside analyst reports.

The Executive Summary's Decisions and Agenda table gathers the open items of every 3P (promise element next to its proof), the proofs to build, the validation tests, the scenario triggers and any section below pass, each with owner, horizon and before or after launch. These items are the input of the Value to Market job.

## Hand-off

Offer Value to Market next, to turn the decisions into an executable plan. Fine-tune is the routine that keeps the strategy alive when a trigger in `Knowledge/Changes and Triggers.md` fires.
